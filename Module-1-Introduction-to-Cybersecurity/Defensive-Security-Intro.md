# Defensive Security Intro

## Objective

Learn how organisations protect systems from cyber attacks.

---

## What is Defensive Security?

Defensive Security focuses on preventing, detecting, investigating, and responding to cyber threats.

Instead of attacking systems, defenders protect them.

---

## Main Goals

- Detect attacks
- Monitor systems
- Respond to incidents
- Protect users
- Reduce risk

---

## Blue Team Responsibilities

- Log Monitoring
- Threat Detection
- Incident Response
- Malware Analysis
- Digital Forensics
- Threat Intelligence

---

## Important Concepts

### SOC

Security Operations Centre

Monitors security alerts 24/7.

---

### SIEM

Security Information and Event Management

Collects logs from multiple devices and generates alerts.

Examples:

- Splunk
- Microsoft Sentinel
- QRadar

---

### Threat Intelligence

Information about attackers, malware, vulnerabilities, and Indicators of Compromise (IOCs).

---

### DFIR

Digital Forensics and Incident Response

Investigates cyber incidents and helps recover systems.

---

### Malware Analysis

Understanding how malicious software behaves.

---

## Common Tools

- Splunk
- Wireshark
- Microsoft Defender
- Velociraptor
- Sysmon

---

## Real-world Example

A SOC analyst notices multiple failed login attempts.

The SIEM generates an alert.

The analyst investigates and blocks the attacker's IP.

---

## What I Learned

- Defence is continuous.
- Logs are extremely important.
- Detection is as important as prevention.
- Blue teams work with many different tools.

---

## Key Takeaways

Good defenders understand attacker techniques.
