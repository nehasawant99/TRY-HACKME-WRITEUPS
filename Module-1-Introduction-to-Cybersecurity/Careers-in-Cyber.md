# Careers in Cyber

## Objective

Understand different cybersecurity career paths.

---

## Major Career Domains

### Offensive Security

Find vulnerabilities before attackers.

Examples:

- Penetration Tester
- Red Team

---

### Defensive Security

Protect systems from attacks.

Examples:

- SOC Analyst
- Incident Responder
- Threat Hunter

---


### Digital Forensics

Investigate cyber crimes and recover evidence.

---

### Malware Analysis

Study malicious software.

---

### Security Engineering

Build secure systems and security infrastructure.

---

### Cloud Security

Protect AWS, Azure and Google Cloud environments.

---
